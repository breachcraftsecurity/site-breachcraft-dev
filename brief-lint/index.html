brief-lint

Refuse the brief before a candidate meets it.

A lab brief is markable or it is filler. This checks the shape and the parts that decide a mark, and exits non-zero so CI can hold the line.

Usage

python -m breachcraft_tools.brief_lint path/to/brief.md [--json]

Exit 0 clean, 1 problems, 2 usage. No third-party packages.

What it refuses

  • A title that is not # <number> <imperative title>, a title over nine words, or one that opens with a gerund.
  • Any of the five sections missing, or present out of order: objective, granted, evidence, marked, time.
  • An objective with more than one sentence or over 40 words.
  • A granted line that does not say what is granted and what is withheld.
  • Evidence that names no concrete artefact - a blob, query, record, hash, subject, policy, count or similar.
  • A marked line too short to say what a pass looks like.
  • A time line without a number and a unit.
  • Banned words: leverage, synergy, cutting-edge, world-class, best-in-class, passionate.

Why each one exists

A brief that fails any of these turns into a discussion at marking time: whose objective was it, what counts as evidence, what does a pass look like. The linter moves that discussion to authoring, where it is cheap.

Limits, stated so a pass is not over-read

It is a shape check, not a judgement. A brief can pass and still be dull, mispriced or aimed at the wrong level. It reads one file at a time and does not know the track, the prerequisites or whether the evidence is actually obtainable in the tenant.
